Safety

Turn around, don't drown. Never drive or walk into flood water. 15 cm (6 in) of moving water can knock you down; 30 cm (1 ft) can sweep a car away.
Smell gas?

Leave immediately on foot. Do not use phones, light switches, vehicles or anything that could make a spark. From a safe distance, call CenterPoint Energy at 1-888-876-5786 and 911.

Flood IQ has no live gas-leak feed. Source: CenterPoint Energy safety guidance.

Power outage

No open outage feed is connected. Check the CenterPoint outage tracker (updates about every 5 minutes). Stay 10 m (35 ft) from downed lines.

Shelters

Open shelters change hour by hour. Use the American Red Cross shelter finder or call 1-800-RED-CROSS, or dial 2-1-1 (United Way Greater Houston).
